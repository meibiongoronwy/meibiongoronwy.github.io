Amdanom Ni / About Us

Cymraeg

Sefydlwyd Côr Meibion Goronwy ym mhentref Benllech yn 2016, gyda'r bwriad o ail-greu diddordeb lleol ym myd y corau meibion.

Ers hynny mae'r côr wedi perfformio ar lwyfannau ledled Gogledd Cymru a thu hwnt, gan gynnwys ymweliadau ag Iwerddon, yr Amwythig a Llundain.

Mae ein repertoire yn cynnwys caneuon traddodiadol Cymraeg a Saesneg ynghyd â darnau mwy cyfoes.

English

Côr Meibion Goronwy was formed in the village of Benllech in 2016 with the aim of renewing local interest in the tradition of male voice singing.

Since then the choir has performed throughout North Wales and further afield, including visits to Ireland, Shrewsbury and London.

Our repertoire ranges from traditional Welsh and English male voice music to more contemporary pieces.
